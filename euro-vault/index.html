02Our story

What the vault holds

Euro Vault is a vault in the oldest sense: a place where good things are kept safe. We collect European destinations that hold up — coast, alpine, city, food — and tell you when to go, what to see, why it matters. No prices. No pressure. Just places worth the trip.

— Euro Vault

03FAQ

Questions & answers

01

When is the best time to go?

Each card names its best season. Between seasons is often quieter — ask us about your dates.

02

How long should I stay?

Two to three days for a city, four or more for coast and alpine routes. Enough to stop rushing.

03

How do I get around?

Trains and ferries link most of these places. For the details of a specific route, contact us and we will tell you.

04

Can I book through Euro Vault?

No. This is inspiration only: no prices, no bookings, no affiliate links.

05

Do you take payment or commission?

Never. Nothing on this page is for sale.
